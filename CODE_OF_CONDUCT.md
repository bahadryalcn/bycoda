# Code of Conduct

We welcome contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Help keep issues, pull requests, and other project discussions respectful and useful.

## Participation

- Discuss ideas and code constructively; avoid personal attacks.
- Assume good intent, ask for clarification, and respect different perspectives.
- Respect privacy. Do not share another person's private information without consent.
- Harassment, discrimination, threats, and abusive behavior are not acceptable.

Technical disagreement and constructive criticism are welcome. Keep feedback
focused on the work and give others room to learn and correct mistakes.

## Reporting and moderation

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact channel listed on the
[maintainer's profile](https://github.com/bahadryalcn). Include relevant context
without posting private information publicly.

Maintainers review concerns in context and respond proportionately. Responses
may include clarification, a request to change behavior, a warning, or participation
restrictions. Serious or repeated misconduct may result in removal from project spaces.
